# @plaza/agreements

Short, plain-language service agreement templates by category (services-small, lodging-str, events, installations), versioned as `id@major.minor`. Muse Wonder fills the scope from the accepted quote; the template controls everything else. Rendered to PDF with Playwright in the EXIMIA design system. Not legal advice; templates reviewed by counsel before v1.0.
